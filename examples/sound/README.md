# sound

Music and sound effects with the Echo sound driver (`main/echo.h`), on a plain
Mega Drive cartridge. See `docs/sound.md` for the driver and its rules.

| Button | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Play or stop the music |
| A | Pause or resume the music |
| B | Sound effect on PSG3, a channel the music leaves free |
| C | Sound effect on FM5, borrowed from the music's chords and given back when it ends |
| Up, Down | Global volume |

The screen shows the music state, the volume and `echo_get_status`: `BGM` while
the music plays (or is paused), `SFX` while an effect plays. Echo's global
volume scales FM and PSG; PCM has no volume, so the drums stay at full level
down to step 4 and are muted below it.

The program reads the controller with the Z80 bus held, as every program using
Echo must (`read_pad` in `src/main.c`).

## The music

`src/song.s` is generated by `tools/gen_song.py`, which holds the song as note
lists, one part per channel, and writes it as an ESF stream, along with the
instrument list and two sound effects. It is a small sequencer in one file, so
it doubles as a worked example of the ESF format. After changing it:

    python3 tools/gen_song.py

The song is an arrangement of "4chords" by Megadev's maintainer
(audiotool.com/track/4chords), under Megadev's license. Its notes and drum
patterns are copied from the original Audiotool project; its synths are
approximated with FM patches built in the generator: an FM bass with a sub
octave, a saw lead, a three-voice chord synth on FM3-5, a two-channel PSG
string fill, PSG noise hats and crash, and PCM kick and snare on FM6. Four bars
of chords play once, then 32 bars loop.

The drum samples in `res/` and the piano of the second sound effect are files
from Echo's repository (github.com/sikthehedgehog/echo, `data/pcm` and
`data/fm`), under Echo's zlib license, `lib/main/echo/LICENSE`.
